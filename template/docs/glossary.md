# Glossary

One row per concept the product shows, one column per locale of `copy.locales` in `.ligeirinho.yml`. A concept has
one term on every screen; a new term goes here before it goes on a screen, and translations come from here
(ligeirinho rules/copy.md).

| Concept | en | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Something to do, with a title and whether it is completed | task | item, todo, entry |
| Marking a task as done (it can't be undone) | complete | finish, close, check |
